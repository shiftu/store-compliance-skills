# Managing Policy Violations and Appeals

[How to handle a policy violation on Google Play](//www.youtube.com/watch?v=xjRqFbTHUOQ)

#### Appealing an Enforcement Action

We will reinstate applications if an error was made, and we find that your application does not violate the Google Play Program Policies and Developer Distribution Agreement. If you’ve reviewed the policies carefully and feel that our decision may have been in error, please follow the instructions provided to you in the enforcement email notification or [click here](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/troubleshooter/2993242) to appeal our decision.

#### Additional Resources

If you are a developer offering apps to users in the European Economic Area (EEA), you may have additional recourse options. See [here](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14659200).

If you need more information regarding an enforcement action or a rating/comment from a user, you may refer to some of the resources below or contact us through the [Google Play Help Center](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer#topic=3450769). We cannot, however, offer you legal advice. If you need legal advice, please consult your legal counsel.
